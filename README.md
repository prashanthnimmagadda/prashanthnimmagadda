## Featured work

### Recall

AI decision intelligence platform for extracting, structuring, and searching decisions from meetings.

The system is designed around speech-to-text, multi-model decision extraction, semantic retrieval, and source-linked decision memory. The product focus is not transcription. It is decision intelligence: what was decided, why it was decided, who was involved, what changed, and what depends on it.

### Lucid

Multi-model AI chat platform with task-based routing across 18+ models.

Lucid is designed around intelligent model selection, token-aware cost controls, fallback cascades, and subscription economics. The core product decision is simple: users should not need to understand model choice for every task.

### Synopse AI

Personalised AI news platform built around RAG, multilingual content processing, and user retention.

Co-founded as Product Lead. Scaled to 5,000+ users across 25 countries, processing 10K+ articles daily in 10 languages, with 42% weekly retention.

### PRD Genie

AI documentation tool for product requirement generation.

Built to reduce PRD writing time by 70% using hybrid retrieval, combining semantic search with keyword retrieval. Designed for practical product teams that need faster, more consistent documentation.

### Review Orchestrator

Multi-agent review system for PRDs, prototypes, and QA workflows.

Designed around structured review outputs, severity scoring, approval checkpoints, and evidence-linked product analysis.

### Stillform

Privacy-first local AI assistant concept focused on Apple Silicon and local-first workflows.

The product direction is simple: personal AI should be private, fast, useful, and grounded in the user’s own files and workflows.

### Fair Credit Intelligence Agent

IBM SkillsBuild AI experiential learning project focused on credit decisioning, explainability, and responsible AI workflows using IBM watsonx.ai and Granite models.
